Privacy — Titan Siege: Tower Defense

Diksapu Games · com.diksapugames.titansiege
Updated: October 24, 2026

Scope

This policy describes internal test version 1.0.0. Ads and purchases are simulated: no real payment or advertising SDK is active.

Progress on your device

The game stores progress, resources, preferences, language and defense plans locally to let you resume your game. There is no user account or progress synchronization with a Diksapu Games server.

Collection and sharing

This version does not transmit personal data to a Diksapu Games server. No analytics or advertising SDK is integrated. The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Support

If you email UnmadeGame@Hotmail.com, your address and message are used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or payment details. Correspondence is retained while the request is handled, unless retention is required.

Deleting data

You can erase your game in the game options or clear app data in Android settings. Uninstalling may also remove local progress. For requests concerning support correspondence, use the email address above.

Platform services and future versions

Google Play and Android may process information for their own services under their own policies. Adding real ads, payments, analytics or online services will require updates to this policy and the disclosures in the game and on Google Play.

Contact
